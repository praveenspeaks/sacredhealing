Manage Slots
Admin → Manage Slots

Slots are the available time windows customers can book. They must exist before anyone can book a session.

1
Add a single slot — Fill in Date, Time, Duration, Price, and Currency in the left panel. Set price to 0 for a free session. Click Add Slot.
2
Repeat / bulk create — Tick Repeat this slot before clicking Add Slot. Choose how many weeks ahead to repeat and which days of the week. Useful for setting up a whole month of Tuesdays at once.
3
Delete a slot — Click the ✕ button on any row in the slots table. Booked slots cannot be deleted — cancel the booking first.
4
Move slots — Use the ↑ ↓ arrow buttons to reorder how slots appear on the public booking calendar.
💡 Slots with price > 0 trigger Stripe payment. Free slots (price = 0) are confirmed immediately without payment. If Stripe is not set up, all bookings are treated as free.
📋
Bookings
Admin → Bookings

View and manage every session booked by customers. Bookings move through a status lifecycle: pending → confirmed → cancelled.

1
View bookings — The table shows each booking with customer name, service, date, time, status badge, and payment amount. Use the status filter dropdown to narrow down the list.
2
Confirm a booking — Click Confirm on a pending booking to mark it confirmed and lock the slot. A confirmation email is sent to the customer automatically.
3
Cancel a booking — Click Cancel to mark it cancelled. The slot is freed up and becomes available for others. For paid bookings, issue refunds manually via your Stripe dashboard.
4
Export — Click Export CSV to download all bookings as a spreadsheet for your records.
⚠️ Paid bookings confirmed via Stripe are auto-confirmed when payment succeeds — you don't need to manually confirm them. Manual confirmation is mainly for free sessions or offline arrangements.
🗂
Bulk Bookings / Packages
Admin → Bulk Bookings  |  Public site → "Book a Package" button

Customers can book 5, 10, 15, or a custom number of sessions in one go and pay once. All slots are locked simultaneously.

1
How customers book a package — On the homepage, click Book a Package › in the hero or next to any service. Or open the booking modal by clicking any time slot, then tick "Book a package of multiple sessions". Choose 5 / 10 / 15 / Custom, fill in details, then pay.
2
View all packages — Go to Admin → Bulk Bookings. Each row shows the customer name, session count, total amount, and status. Click a row to expand and see every individual slot included.
3
Release a slot — If a customer wants to change one of their booked times, click Release next to that slot. The slot becomes free on the calendar. The bulk booking status changes to partial.
4
Rebook a slot — After releasing, click Rebook on the same row. A panel appears with a dropdown of all available future slots. Select the customer's preferred new time and click Confirm Rebook.
5
Refunds — If payment was taken but all slots become unavailable, the system automatically issues a full Stripe refund and marks the booking as refunded. Manual refunds for partial changes must be processed in your Stripe dashboard.

Site Content
Admin → Site Content

Edit every piece of text that appears on the public website — hero, about, contact, footer, the 6 R's cards, and the How It Works steps.

1
Edit any text — Type directly into any field. All text boxes are live-editable. Click 💾 Save Changes at the top-right when done. Changes appear on the website instantly.
2
Homepage Texts — Change the large hero title, subtitle, and the two button labels (Begin Your Journey and Explore Services).
3
About Section — Update the About title and two body paragraphs shown on the homepage About block and the About page.
4
Contact & Footer Info — Update your email address, phone number, location, Instagram URL, WhatsApp link, footer tagline, and copyright text. These appear in the footer and contact sections on every page.
5
6 R's of Sacred Healing™ — Edit the title, pull-quote, and description for each of the 6 cards (Recognise, Release, Restore, Realign, Reclaim, Radiate). These appear on the homepage, Philosophy page, and About page.
6
How It Works — Edit the three journey steps and the "Not Sure?" guidance section and its button label.
💡 Content is shared across all pages automatically. Changing the hero title here updates it on the homepage, and changing footer info updates it on every page simultaneously.

Images & Backgrounds
Admin → Site Content → Image Uploads section

Upload photos for your site logo, healer photos, and section background images. All uploads are applied instantly — no page reload needed.

1
Site Logo — Upload a PNG or SVG logo. Appears in the top-left navbar and footer on every page.
2
Healer Photo — Hero section — The photo that appears next to the main title on the homepage hero.
3
Healer Photo — About section — A separate photo for the About page and the About section on the homepage. Can be the same image or a different one.
4
Section Background Images — Upload an optional atmospheric background image for any section (Hero, About, 6 R's, Services, How It Works, Testimonials). A dark overlay is applied automatically so text stays readable. Leave empty to use the default solid colour.

6 R's Icon Images — Upload a custom photo or icon for each of the 6 R cards. These replace the default illustrated SVG icons. Leave empty to keep the default illustration.
💡 Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP. Maximum file size: 5 MB. Images are stored permanently and survive server restarts and redeployments.

Services
Admin → Services

Add, edit, reorder, and delete the healing services offered on the site. Each service gets its own card on the homepage and its own detail page.

1
Add a service — Fill in the form on the left: title, description, duration, price, and up to 6 bullet-point features. Click Save Service. The service appears immediately on the public website.
2
Edit a service — Click the pencil ✎ icon next to any service in the table. The form loads with existing values — update and click Save Service.
3
Reorder services — Use the ↑ Up and ↓ Down buttons to change the order services appear on the homepage and services page. The order saves automatically.
4
Add an emoji icon — Click the smiley face button next to the Icon field to open the icon picker. Select any emoji to use as the service card icon.
5
Service detail page — Each service automatically gets its own URL at /services/service-title. Customers can visit it directly or be linked there from the service card "Learn More" button.
6
Delete a service — Click the ✕ Delete button. This removes the service from the database and the public website immediately. This action cannot be undone.

Reviews
Admin → Reviews  |  Public site → Testimonials section → "Share Your Journey"

Customers can submit reviews from the public site. All reviews are held for your approval before appearing on the website — you decide what goes live.

1
How customers submit a review — On the homepage, scroll to the Testimonials section and click Share Your Journey. They fill in their name and experience and submit. The review is sent to you for approval.
2
Approve a review — Go to Admin → Reviews. Pending reviews are listed at the top. Click Approve to make it visible on the public testimonials carousel immediately.
3
Reject a review — Click Delete to permanently remove a review that is not appropriate or relevant.
4
Approved reviews appear — Once approved, the review joins the testimonials carousel on the homepage and the dedicated Testimonials page. It auto-rotates with other reviews.

FAQs
Admin → FAQ  |  Public site → /faq.html

Manage the Frequently Asked Questions that appear on the public FAQ page. You can add, edit, delete, and reorder questions.

1
Add an FAQ — Type the question and answer into the form at the top of the FAQ page. Click Add FAQ. It appears on the public FAQ page instantly.
2
Edit an FAQ — Click the pencil ✎ icon next to any question. Update the text and save.
3
Reorder FAQs — Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to move questions up or down. The most important questions are typically shown first.
4
Delete an FAQ — Click the ✕ button to permanently remove a question.
5
Rich text in answers — FAQ answers support basic formatting: wrap text in **bold** for bold, start lines with - for bullet points. Links use standard HTML: <a href="...">link text</a>.

Navigation & Section Control
Admin → Navigation

Control which sections appear in the header and footer menus, whether they live on the homepage or their own page, and what order they appear in.

1
Section location — Home vs Own Page — Each section can either live on the homepage (scrolls to it via anchor) or have its own dedicated URL (e.g. /about, /services). Change this in the Location column dropdown.
2
Show in Header Nav — Tick this checkbox to show the section link in the top navigation bar. Untick to hide it from the header.
3
Show in Footer Nav — Tick to show the link in the footer navigation. Header and footer can be controlled independently.
4
Reorder sections — Drag rows using the ⠿ handle on the left to change the order sections appear in the navigation menus. Lower position = further left in the header.
5
Menu labels — Edit the text shown in the navigation menu in the Menu Label column. For example, change "About" to "Our Story".
6
Save — Click 💾 Save Changes. The navigation updates on the live site immediately for all visitors.
💡 If a section is set to Own Page, it gets a direct URL (e.g. /philosophy) and is removed from the homepage flow. Set it back to Home to reintegrate it into the single-page experience.

Theme & Fonts
Admin → Theme & Fonts

Customise the look of the entire website — change the heading and body fonts, and the accent colour used for buttons, highlights, and decorative elements.

1
Heading Font — Choose a font for all titles, section headers, and pull-quotes. Click any font tile to select it. The live preview on the right updates instantly.
2
Body Font — Choose a font for paragraphs, buttons, and navigation. A different body font from the heading creates visual contrast and sophistication.
3
Accent Colour — Click any colour swatch to change the accent. This colour is used for buttons, gold star ratings, decorative lines, and hover effects across the whole site.
4
Save — Click 💾 Save Theme. The font and colour changes go live on the public website immediately.
💡 The live preview card on the right shows exactly how your chosen heading font, body font, and accent colour will look together before you save.

System Settings
Admin → Settings

View the live status of Stripe payments and email notifications. Configuration is done via the .env file on the server.

1
Stripe Payments — The green/red indicator shows whether Stripe is active. Green = paid bookings go through Stripe checkout. Red = all bookings are free (no payment taken). To enable Stripe, add STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_live_... to your .env file and restart the server.
2
Test vs Live mode — Use sk_test_ keys while testing. Switch to sk_live_ when ready for real payments. Never mix test and live keys.
3
Email Notifications — Green = booking confirmation emails are sent to customers and alert emails are sent to you. Red = emails are disabled. To enable, set all SMTP settings in .env: SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT, SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASS, SMTP_FROM, and ADMIN_EMAIL.
4
Change admin password — Update the ADMIN_PASSWORD value in the .env file and restart the server. Use a strong, unique password for the live site.
⚠️ After any .env change, the server must be restarted for the change to take effect. Contact your developer if you are unsure how to do this.
