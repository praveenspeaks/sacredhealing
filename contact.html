✦ Cancellation Policy

Rescheduling & Cancellations

We understand that life is unpredictable. If you need to cancel or reschedule your session, we ask for at least 24 hours' notice so that the time can be offered to someone else who needs it.

Sessions cancelled with 48 or more hours' notice will receive a full refund or session credit. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed sessions without prior contact, may not be eligible for a refund or rescheduling.

To reschedule, please reach out as early as possible. We will always do our best to find an alternative time that works for you.

We appreciate your understanding — thoughtful cancellations help us serve all clients with the care and dedication they deserve.
